Three checks, all must hold. First, the scoreboard shows at least one cap as active (the status column says CAP, or a note says "cap" or "capped") and the response gives the reason for it somewhere, in any length. Second, the response does not create a file and does not say it created one; offering to write files later if the user changes their mind is fine. Third, the response asks at most two numbered or lettered questions of the user. Answer options listed under a question do not count as questions, and rhetorical phrasing inside explanations does not count.
